Transparent Fare Freedom

AI suggests the fair corridor. Drivers still own their price.

MaiRide does not force rigid company-fixed fares onto independent drivers. Our AI engine studies route distance, corridor demand, live market signals, and historical trip behavior to suggest an optimal fare range, while drivers retain the freedom to set the price that works for their vehicle economics.
